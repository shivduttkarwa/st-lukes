## 2026-10-29 — menu rise settle
- Main menu row divider is a 1px `::after` line instead of an inset `box-shadow`. With GPU raster at fractional scales (panel zoom × 125% Windows scaling) the shadow left a faint sliver down the row's right edge, which read as a frame round the open (royal) row; Figma has only the bottom line (`20169:15818`). Geometry unchanged.
- Opening a row turns its corner arrow 90° clockwise over 0.6s (↗ to ↘) instead of flipping it with `scaleY(-1)`; the open frame's arrow is a rotated instance too. Closing turns it back.
- The turn is a registered `--menu-turn` angle driving `rotate()` on the SVG's `<use>`. A `transform` transition ran on a GPU layer: the arrow wobbled ±1px while turning and jumped ~0.75px when it landed (most visible on the previous row's arrow turning back). Now it repaints crisp each frame and settles without a jump.
- Submenu items fade in place, no movement: in over 0.5s, 42ms apart; out over 0.3s, 28ms apart. The fade-in runs on a registered `--menu-alpha` (main thread) instead of `opacity` keyframes: an opacity animation gets a GPU layer, and on the first open the panel widens under it, so each item landed ~0.6px off and jumped when its fade ended. Now ≤0.12px at 1×, which is ClearType switching on at full opacity rather than movement. On a row switch the old column fades out before the new one fades in. The fade moved from the column onto the items and thumbnail, and the column only hides once they have gone. Same on the phone accordion. Reduced motion swaps columns instantly.
- Submenu thumbnail swap rewritten: it compared each hover with the first `<img>` in the box, which mid-cross-fade was the outgoing one, so quick hovers were skipped and the wrong image could stay up. The script now tracks the image actually shown, decodes the new one before showing it, drops any that a later hover has superseded, and prunes older layers once the newest has landed. Reopening a submenu resets to its first child, as in Figma. The transition is ported from the St Patrick's cutup's menu: the old image fades out while zooming to 110%, and the new one fades in from 110% down to 100% (St Patrick's uses 150%; reduced on request), both over 0.9s on `--ease-in-out-soft` (GSAP `power1.inOut`). A hover mid-swap first completes the swap in progress, then starts the next. The thumbnail is now one shared element for the whole menu (same place: bottom of the submenu column), so switching top-level rows cross-fades the same way to the new row's first child image; opening a submenu from closed keeps the delayed fade-in. Stress-tested with 60 random hovers at 30–500ms gaps on two submenus: every settled image matched the last hovered row.
- The middle utility link (Enquire) now unfolds and fades in with the panel as it widens, and folds away as it narrows: its own grid column goes 0fr → 1fr and its leading gap is cancelled with a matching negative margin, on the panel's 0.7s `--ease-in-out`. It used to switch from `display: none` in one frame, so the search pill jumped and then grew. The label sits in a `<span>` clipped horizontally only (`overflow-x: clip`), so the link's 44px tap area and the Q's tail survive. Resting positions unchanged at 1440 × 1024 (narrow: search 195.9, Tours 1171.9, Enrol 1248.2; wide: search 432.0, Tours 1079.2, Enquire 1155.5).
- Every main menu row now has 3–4 placeholder children (labels and thumbnails are dummy, pending the client's sitemap), so each submenu can be reviewed.
- Risen menu items keep `will-change: translate` while the menu (or submenu) is open, and the panel keeps `will-change: transform`. Chrome draws an animating layer at a whole-pixel offset, and every position here is fractional (fluid rem, panel zoom), so each row sat ~0.35px high during the rise and dropped when its animation ended. Holding the rows' layers alone left them stale once the panel's slide ended or it widened (text soft, and shifting ~0.3px on first hover); with the panel held too they sit at a fixed offset inside it. A main-thread rise was tried and dropped: text then moves in whole-pixel steps and visibly shivers. The held layers are dropped the first time a submenu widens the panel (`has-widened`, cleared on each open): a layered panel sits on whole pixels while its width animates, so everything pinned to its right edge (Tours / Enquire / Enrol, close) wobbled ~1px through the widen. Without layers they hold still; the one sub-half-pixel settle lands in the click's first frame.
- Checked by frame capture at 1440 × 900 (1× and 1.25×) and 390 × 844 (2×, 3×): rise and submenu rise glide to rest with no drop, no first-hover shift, phone rows below an opened accordion unchanged when repainted. Cost: at 100% Windows scaling the open menu's text is greyscale-smoothed instead of ClearType (no difference at 125% and up).

## 2026-10-28 — menu motion
- Panel glides in over 0.8s on one in-out curve (`--ease-in-out`) as the scrim fades; it leaves in 0.6s. Contents fade up 14px in order once it is moving: search / utility links / close, then each nav row 40ms apart, then the small links and social buttons. Opening a submenu staggers its children the same way and fades the thumbnail in with a slow 5% settle. Short moves on a gentle curve only; no masks, no colour layers.

- Closing keeps the scroll lock (and Lenis stopped) until the panel has left, timed from the menu's own CSS delay; before, the scrollbar came back at once and the fixed panel jumped 15px mid-slide.
- Burger and close (`20169:15273`, `20169:15464`) checked: both 65 gold circles at x 1325, y 30 (50 from the right), 27 after Enrol. Both icons are now one CSS `.menu-icon` (two 2px bars, 30 wide, 10 apart, the pair 1px above centre as in Figma; the build's burger had been 1px low). The close is always the X; it stays invisible while the panel slides in, fades in from 450ms, and fades out in 200ms as the panel leaves. The sprite's `icon-menu` and `icon-close` are gone.
- Thin scrollbar site-wide (`scrollbar-width: thin`, navy-tint thumb on a clear track; 10px on Windows instead of 15), the menu panel's too. The thumb colour is a registered `--scrollbar-thumb`, transparent while locked, so it fades back in over 0.6s when the lock lifts instead of snapping.
